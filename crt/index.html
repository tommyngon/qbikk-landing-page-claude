QBIKK on CRT

Click a demo. Preview adds a simulated CRT look for your laptop. CRT mode is the plain picture for the real tube.

Keys inside a demo
  • C switch Preview / CRT mode   G safe-area guides (preview only)   F fullscreen   H hide the help text
  • Show on the real CRT: open a demo with ?mode=crt (the CRT buttons do this), then press F.
